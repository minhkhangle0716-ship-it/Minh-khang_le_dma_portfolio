Alien Angler

Underwater Fishing Game

Alien Angler is a p5.js game where you catch the fish you want to showcase in your aquarium/ homescreen!

Game Design

This was my biggest coding project yet. Using the skills learned in Art 101, I wanted to make a minigame that also fit the ocean theme I was doing with my previous projects. I started by gathering reference and creating a wire frame, so that I knew how to code the game in sections. I hand made the backgrounds and title using Procreate. The unique gimic I included to make it different from other fishing games, is that the fish you catch will swim around on your home screen. There are also different biomes where unique effects happen to your rod and fish.
