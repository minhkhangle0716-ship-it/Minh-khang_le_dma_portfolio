Artist Statement
Minh-khang Le is a concept artist for characters and environments based in San Jose, California. Growing up in the Bay Area, his work is influenced by the diverse culture and climate, shaping the worlds they create. Growing up, Minh experienced countless characters and settings through digital mediums, like animation and video games. This interest has evolved from crayon drawings, to animation, concept art, and interactive media, always keeping in mind your senses to create immersive environments.

Currently a Digital Media Art major at San Jose State University, Minh looks to explore different mediums to improve his art. In his classes he furthers his skills with Adobe apps, coding, and Blender.

After becoming a recipient of the San Jose Downtown Doors 2024 award with his digital illustration (shown above), Minh works to contribute to the city's visual culture. His long term goal is to become an concept artist for games and shows, where he can bring characters and environments to life using technology, and visual design.
