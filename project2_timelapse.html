Underwater Timelapse

This p5.js animation follows an underwater world through four stages: tropical surface, ocean, deep sea, and abyss. Waves, fish, seaweed, bubbles, and particles build the changing environments as the palette shifts from bright surface water to the dark depths.

Animation

In this project I learned how to make an animation that focuses on having different acts, and can play out without input. Continuing from my fish from example 4, and sine recording in class, I started with a mash of a bunch of effects to see what would happen. Most of my effects were from the class website, like the particles and proximity links. Early on I wanted to incorporate the ocean theme into my different acts, so I made each act go deeper underwater, from the surface to the ocean floor. To do this I spent a lot of time testing out the fish colors to see which color combinations worked well. Eventually I learned about lerpColor() which I used to blend in colors a certain amount per act. I think the final turned out pretty calm and relaxing. I am proud of how the last acts turned out. The glow effects from the fish and background makes a relaxing background to look at. To improve I could have added more passive action, like bigger things flowing through the canvas, or a more engaging background
