Art 13 Projects

1 Cut 2 Folds Draft

In this project we explored making a complex 3D shape with as minimally as possible. Using just 1 sheet of cardboard, 1 cut and 2 folds, we explored what makes a 3d form appealing and engaging. I mostly focused on not making a full side of the figure lay on the ground because that creates a sense of too much stability, removing the flow and dynamism of the piece.

1 Cut 2 Folds Final

Using a sheet of aluminum, I recreated the cardboard project. I polished the surface with sandpaper and used workshop tools in the art building. I focused on making the piece look good in any angle, and important thing to learn about 3d art and silhouettes.

Wordplay project

We were assigned to create a piece themed around a word or phrase of our choosing. I was looking forward to summer break, so I had beaches on my mind. To narrow down the theme, I chose "tide pools". Using styrofoam, plaster, and resin, I created a display that both spells out the words and resembles one. Even though it was 3d art, I saw the resemblence in steps to 2d art. It often starts with a base or sketch, then layered on with paint and details.
