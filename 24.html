Art 24 Pieces

In these exercises, I was proud of how the glass rendering turned out. Using just charcoal and paper, this class taught me how to focus on values and rendering objects simplistically but effectively.

I tested out rendering different materials, like a metallic surface of a watering can. I learned the importance of hard surfaces and which areas to blend and not to blend to avoid muddyness.

One of the last exercises was to render a piece of cloth. I focused on the folds and creases, and how they create shapes and values. Again, the importance of choosing what is a hard edge and what is a soft edge was important to make the piece look more realistic.
