Storytelling Project

Storyboard

In this project I did something similar to my montage project, where it covered a child's growth into adulthood. I covered my own artist journey on top of that, as well as the doubts of becoming an artist. Artist work so hard on their skills, but with the changes to society, it's becoming more difficult to make a living as an artist. This project allowed me to explore these themes and create a narrative that reflects the challenges and rewards of pursuing a career in the arts. I wanted to show all the work it takes to become an artist, but also the beauty of it. I hope that this video can inspire others to pursue their artistic passions, because in the face of generative AI, there is still so much you can create which AI can't.

To begin the project, I went through all my childhood art, going down memory lane. It was beautiful seeing how the themes and subjects in my pieces have often stayed the same to the art I make today.

While making this project in Premiere Pro, I used a lot of keyframes because most of the media I used were pictures, and I had to give it some kind of movement. For older footage, I used a warm yellow highlight with cooler shadows, while more modern art had cooler blue highlights with warmer shadow. I really liked how I was able to explore the similarities of my old art with the work I do today, especially in the last minute of the project, where there were direct comparisons.
